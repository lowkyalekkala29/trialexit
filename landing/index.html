FAQ

Questions, answered

Does TrialExit cancel subscriptions for me?

No — and nothing that does it without your password should be trusted. We open the exact cancel page and show the steps, so cancelling takes about a minute.

Do you read my emails?

Only if you choose "Scan my Gmail", and only to find trial confirmation emails. Access is read-only, emails are read inside your browser and never uploaded. You can disconnect any time.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — the web app works on any phone and sends reminders by email. The Chrome extension (automatic checkout detection) is for computers.

Which services are supported?

Any service with a free trial. For popular ones like Netflix, Spotify, YouTube Premium, ChatGPT, Prime Video, JioHotstar, Adobe and Canva we also have direct cancel links and steps.

What if a cancel link is wrong?

Every link shows when it was last checked. Report it and we fix it for everyone — no app update needed.

How much does it cost?

Free during launch, and early users keep it free. Later there'll be a one-time lifetime unlock — never a subscription.
