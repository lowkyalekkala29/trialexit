Questions

Do you connect to my bank?

No, and we never will. TrialExit stores the service, end date and amount — no card numbers, no bank login.

Why do you need my email?

It's optional. Every trial sends a confirmation email — even ones started on your phone or in an app store — so your inbox is the only place that sees all of them. Access is read-only, searches only for trial emails, and the emails are read inside the extension on your device. They are never uploaded: TrialExit has no server. Disconnect any time.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser, on your device. There is no account and no server holding your subscription list.

What if the cancel link is out of date?

Every link shows when it was last verified, and a report button files an issue. The directory is a public file, so fixes ship as pull requests rather than app releases.

Does it work on every site?

Yes. The extension watches for trial checkouts on any website and pops up "Trial started — track it?" the moment you sign up, with the end date and price filled in. Ordinary pages are skipped after a quick check on your device; nothing you browse is uploaded or stored. You can mute a site or turn it off.

Why a calendar export as well as a notification?

The calendar file is what follows you to your phone. If the extension lives on your laptop, the alert still lands in the calendar app you actually look at.

Does it cancel for me?

No — and nothing that does so without your password should be trusted. It takes you to the exact page and tells you whether that service needs chat support.
