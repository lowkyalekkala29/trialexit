## The service
TrialExit helps you keep track of free trials and reminds you before they renew. It shows links and steps for cancelling with each service. **TrialExit never cancels anything on your behalf** and is not affiliated with any service it lists.

## Your account
You sign in with Google, Microsoft or a one-time email code. Keep access to that email account secure. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Data & privacy; this permanently deletes your data from our servers.

We work hard to send every reminder on time, but email delivery, notifications and third-party cancel pages are outside our full control. **You remain responsible for cancelling subscriptions you don't want.** Always check the renewal date with the service itself. ## Cancel links
Cancellation links and steps are community-maintained and may go out of date. Each link shows when it was last verified. Report broken links and we'll fix them.
